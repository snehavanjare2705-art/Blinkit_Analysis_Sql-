## 📊 Blinkit Business Analysis | MySQL, Advanced SQL

### 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed 3.85M+ Blinkit transaction records using MySQL to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, revenue performance, and delivery efficiency. The project addresses business challenges related to understanding sales performance, identifying high-value customers, and improving operational decision-making.

### 🎯 Business Problems Solved

* **Sales & Revenue Analysis:** Examined order trends, revenue patterns, and product performance to identify high-performing products and sales opportunities.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzed customer purchasing behavior to identify high-value customer groups and support targeted marketing and retention strategies.
* **Order Trend Analysis:** Evaluated order patterns to understand purchasing trends and support inventory planning and demand forecasting.
* **Delivery Performance:** Examined delivery-related metrics to identify operational patterns and potential areas for improvement.
* **Business Performance Monitoring:** Used SQL queries to answer 20+ business questions and generate insights that support data-driven decisions.

### 💡 Business Usefulness

* Helps businesses understand revenue patterns and identify opportunities to improve sales.
* Supports customer retention and targeted promotional strategies through customer segmentation.
* Helps with inventory planning by identifying product demand and purchasing trends.
* Highlights potential delivery inefficiencies and opportunities to improve operational performance.
* Enables data-driven decision-making through structured SQL analysis and reusable queries.

### 🛠️ Tools & SQL Concepts

* **Database:** MySQL
* **SQL Concepts:** CTEs, Window Functions, Joins, Views, CASE Statements, Aggregate Functions, Subqueries, and GROUP BY.
* **Analysis Areas:** Revenue Analysis, Customer Segmentation, Sales Trends, Product Performance, and Delivery Performance.
